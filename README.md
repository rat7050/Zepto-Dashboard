# 🛵 Zepto Sales & Delivery Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Zepto's sales, customer behavior, delivery performance, and profitability. The dashboard helps identify key business challenges, uncover actionable insights, and support data-driven decision-making.

The project focuses on analyzing order trends, revenue generation, customer demographics, delivery efficiency, and product category performance across multiple states in India.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Zepto aims to improve operational efficiency and business growth by addressing:

- Uneven revenue distribution across states
- High average delivery time
- Order cancellations and delivery issues
- Customer retention challenges
- Demand fluctuations across product categories
- Inventory and resource optimization

## 📈 Key Business Insights

### Revenue Concentration
- Maharashtra generates the highest revenue.
- Other states present growth opportunities.

### Delivery Performance
- Average delivery time is 41.3 minutes.
- Faster deliveries can improve customer satisfaction and retention.

### Customer Behavior
- 10K customers generated 20K orders.
- Average customer places approximately 2 orders.

### Order Trends
- Monthly orders fluctuate significantly.
- Demand forecasting can improve inventory planning.

### Profitability
- Revenue: ₹132M
- Profit: ₹96M
- Profit Margin: 72.7%

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

### Improve Delivery Efficiency
- Optimize delivery routes.
- Increase delivery partners during peak hours.
- Expand dark store coverage.

### Increase Revenue
- Target low-performing states with promotional campaigns.
- Focus on regional marketing strategies.

### Reduce Order Cancellations
- Improve inventory tracking.
- Enhance order fulfillment processes.

### Improve Customer Retention
- Launch loyalty programs.
- Provide personalized offers and recommendations.

### Optimize Inventory
- Forecast demand using historical order patterns.
- Maintain safety stock for high-demand products.
